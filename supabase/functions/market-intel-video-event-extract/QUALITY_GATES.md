# Creator video event quality gates

Model extraction is intentionally followed by deterministic guards.

- A linked decklist is identity context, never evidence by itself.
- Routine casts and ordinary play-by-play do not graduate to Signals.
- Basic lands are rejected unless the creator is explicitly evaluating that land choice.
- Competitive deckbuilding judgments such as `mandatory`, `had success with`, `really powerful`, explicit synergy, copy-count changes, and keep/cut decisions may graduate.
- Raw transcript mentions and the card resolution method/confidence are retained for auditability.
- Transcript acquisition remains cache-first and Supadata `mode=native`; event reprocessing must not spend another transcript credit.
